# FIX-007 — normative value: the obligation is not the commitment

> **Fictional worked fixture.** Ashworth Building Society is invented; the regulation is described
> generically.

**Proves:** obligations generate commitments, and the distinction survives interpretation — the
regulation is authority-relevant evidence; the organisation's undertaking in response is the OVC.

## Sources

**A — Compliance briefing, `outcome-duty-briefing.md`, dated 2026-02-06**

> The consumer outcome duty requires firms to demonstrate that product changes leave no identified
> customer cohort with materially worse outcomes.

**B — Product committee paper, `overdraft-restructure-paper.md`, dated 2026-02-20**

> In restructuring the overdraft proposition we commit to demonstrating, cohort by cohort, that no
> identified customer group is left materially worse off — measured against the January cohort
> outcome baseline — with evidence assembled quarterly for the duty. Chief Product Officer Daniel
> Awotwi is accountable; reconciliation at the FY-end outcomes review.

## Expected

- **One CandidateAssembly** for the organisation's undertaking (B): commitment (demonstrable no-worse
  outcomes per cohort) · beneficiary (identified customer cohorts) · baseline (January cohort
  outcomes) · committed-by (Daniel Awotwi) · due-point (FY-end review) · recognition-route (quarterly
  evidence assembly).
- **A → `authority` evidence** for that candidate: the duty is what binds the organisation. The
  briefing yields no candidate of its own.
- `value-kind` reasoned to `risk-reduction`, reading stated.
- `missing_semantics`: `finance-countersignatory`, `falsifying-signal`, `linked-decision` (the
  restructure decision is referenced but not identified — the reference is noted, the id reported
  missing).

## Must not

- **Record the regulation as the commitment.** A duty stated in a briefing is not an organisational
  undertaking; only B commits anyone.
- Produce a candidate from A alone.
- Treat "for the duty" as the beneficiary. The cohorts are the beneficiary; the duty is authority.
- Infer the linked decision's id from the paper's title.
