# FIX-011 — a KPI is not a commitment

> **Fictional worked fixture.** Ashworth Building Society is invented.

**Proves:** a KPI target with nobody bound to it yields `measure` contributions at most, and **no
candidate**. A number nobody committed to is the shadow of a claim.

## Source

**A — Performance pack, `q2-performance-pack.md`**

> Dashboard targets for H2: complaints per 1,000 accounts ≤ 3.2 · digital adoption 64% → 70% ·
> cost-income ratio 61% → 58%. RAG status against each is reported monthly to the performance
> forum.

Nothing else. No owner of any target, no authority establishing them, no beneficiary, no linked
decision — a reporting artefact.

## Expected

- `measure` contributions for the three targets, with `epistemic_confidence` low — a dashboard
  asserts targets exist, not that anyone undertook them.
- **Zero CandidateAssemblies.** The output states why: measures without a `commitment` contribution
  bind nobody, and the boundary test — value ≠ KPI — names the gap.
- The report may note these measures as *awaiting* a commitment they could evidence, clearly
  separated from any claim one exists.

## Must not

- Construct three candidate commitments from three targets.
- Promote the performance forum to a committer or the RAG process to a recognition route.
- Infer beneficiaries from the metric subjects (complainants, digital users).
- Return nothing at all — the measures are real contributions; it is the commitment that is absent.
