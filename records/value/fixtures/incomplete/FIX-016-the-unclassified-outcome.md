# FIX-016 — the unclassified outcome

> **Fictional worked fixture.** Meridian Mutual is invented.

**Proves:** the vocabulary's own escape valve. Material genuinely relevant to Value that fits none
of the thirteen contribution classes is reported as the **`unclassified` recovery outcome** — not
forced into a near-fitting class, not dropped, and not made a fourteenth role.

## Source

**A — Strategy appendix, `platform-optionality-note.md`, dated 2026-04-22**

> The open-banking platform investment is justified less by its direct returns than by the options
> it creates: each integration we build preserves our ability to enter adjacent propositions within
> a quarter rather than a year, whether or not we ever exercise any given option. The Board regards
> this optionality itself as something the Society now holds and should not give up cheaply.

The material is unmistakably about value the organisation holds and intends to keep. But it is not
a `commitment` to an outcome, not `protection` of an existing outcome (the options are new, and
nothing states what must not degrade), not a `measure`, and not `context` for another contribution
— it asserts a *kind* of value (option value) the current vocabulary has no class for.

## Expected

- A `SourceFragment` for the passage, captured verbatim.
- **The `unclassified` recovery outcome reported**: the fragment reference, what was recognised
  ("an asserted organisational holding of option value, Board-regarded, with a keep-it stance"),
  and why classification failed against the nearest classes — `protection` considered and rejected
  with the reason.
- **Zero contributions carrying a forced class**, and zero candidates.
- The report notes that a recurring pattern of this shape is evidence for a vocabulary decision —
  the TDR route, stated.

## Must not

- Classify as `protection` because "should not give up cheaply" pattern-matches preservation. The
  nearest-fit temptation is the failure under test.
- Classify as `commitment` — nothing is undertaken, no outcome is stated.
- Emit a contribution with an invented `optionality` role.
- Drop the passage silently. Unclassified is a first-class outcome, and losing the observation
  loses the evidence the vocabulary needs to evolve.
