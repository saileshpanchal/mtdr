# VR-9302 — Depot running-cost reduction

> **Fictional.** Brackwell District Council is invented. This record exists to be assessed, not read.

**The state under assessment:** the same candidate, now counter-signed. Every machine-verifiable
prerequisite for ratification is satisfied — and it is still a candidate, because eligibility is not
ratification and no human has acted.

## The commitment

Consolidating three operational depots onto one site, to reduce annual depot running costs for the
General Fund, committed by the Director of Environment and Place under Cabinet's approval of
18 February 2026.

## Baseline

£1.42m annual depot running cost, 2025/26, from the FY26 revenue outturn, cost centres 4410–4412.

## Intended outcome and recognition

£0.98m by 2027/28. Recognised in the Medium Term Financial Plan from FY28 and visible in cost centre
4410 once the other two centres close.

**Falsifying signal:** combined running costs above £1.15m at the end of FY27.
