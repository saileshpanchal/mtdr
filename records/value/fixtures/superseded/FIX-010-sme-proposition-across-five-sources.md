# FIX-010 — the SME proposition: aspiration, binding, constraint, and quiet supersession

> **Fictional worked fixture.** Meridian Mutual is invented.

**Proves:** the canonical scattered case — an ambition in strategy, success defined elsewhere, a
constraint in a risk paper, the beneficiary in research, and a later paper quietly replacing the
original commitment. Recovery must assemble it, and must recognise the replacement as supersession.

## Sources

**A — Strategy, `strategy-2026.md`, dated 2026-01-20**
> We will become the leading community bank for small businesses in our region.

**B — Board paper, `sme-success-measures.md`, dated 2026-02-18**
> Success for the SME proposition, as agreed: SME deposit growth of 15% and a relationship-NPS above
> 40 by FY28, from the FY25 positions (deposits £310m; NPS 22, autumn wave). Commercial Director
> Sofia Andersson is accountable to the Board for the proposition.

**C — Risk appetite note, `risk-appetite-h1.md`, dated 2026-03-05**
> SME growth is pursued within the existing sector concentration limit; no single sector may exceed
> 20% of the SME book.

**D — Customer research summary, `sme-research-q1.md`, dated 2026-03-12**
> The proposition's intended beneficiaries are trading small businesses in the region — not
> intermediaries, and not the property-investment segment.

**E — ExCo paper, `exco-2026-06-09.md`**
> Following the funding review, the SME proposition is refocused: the deposit-growth ambition is
> replaced by a relationship-depth target (products per SME relationship, 1.8 → 2.4 by FY28). The
> Board's February accountability arrangements continue.

## Expected

- **One CandidateAssembly**: A → `commitment` (aspiration; **`derivation: explicit` but the
  commitment reading is only admissible because B binds it** — an aspiration hardened by later
  authority); B → `success`, `measure`, `time`, `committed-by` (Sofia Andersson), baselines;
  C → `constraint`; D → `beneficiary` (with the exclusions preserved); E → `supersession`.
- **One entry in `supersessions`**: the deposit-growth target superseded by the relationship-depth
  target, basis E's explicit replacement language *plus* the continued Board accountability
  ("the February arrangements continue") — shared authority lineage, not recency.
- **The NPS measure survives** — E replaces only the deposit ambition.
- Completeness view: `commitment` supported · `success`/`measure` supported (post-supersession
  values) · `beneficiary` supported · constraint recorded · `finance-countersignatory` missing ·
  `falsifying-signal` missing.

## Must not

- Reject A as generic aspiration and lose the chain. Alone it fails the boundary test; with B it is
  the commitment's statement. Identification reports, reconciliation assembles.
- Treat E as a conflict, or as a second commitment. The lineage is explicit.
- Drop C's constraint or D's exclusions — vanished constraints are a challenge finding.
- Backfill the superseded deposit target as still current, or delete it — both values persist with
  their ordering.
