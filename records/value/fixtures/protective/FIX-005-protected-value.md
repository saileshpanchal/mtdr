# FIX-005 — protected value: preservation is fully a commitment

> **Fictional worked fixture.** Ashworth Building Society is invented.

**Proves:** *pursuit ≠ protection*, and both are commitments. A commitment to **preserve** an outcome
is an OVC in its own right — not a condition, footnote or constraint on a growth commitment.

## Sources

**A — Migration business case, `branch-migration-case.md`, dated 2026-04-14**

> The migration releases £1.1m in annual property cost. Baseline: current estate cost £4.8m,
> FY25 ledger.

**B — Board condition, `board-minutes-2026-04-30.md`**

> Approved, on the condition that vulnerable-customer access outcomes are maintained at current
> levels throughout: the access survey score (78, March 2026 wave) is not to fall during migration,
> and the Board asked for this to be tracked as a commitment in its own right, owned by
> Customer Director Amara Okonkwo.

## Expected

- **Two CandidateAssemblies**: a pursuit commitment (the £1.1m release, beneficiary the Society) and
  a **preservation commitment** (access outcomes maintained, beneficiary vulnerable customers, baseline
  the 78 score with its wave date, `committed-by` Amara Okonkwo, authority the Board's condition).
- The two candidates **linked by context** — the preservation is the pursuit's review boundary *and*
  a commitment in its own right, exactly as B states.
- The preservation candidate's `value-kind` reasoning lands on `risk-reduction` (protection of an
  existing outcome), with the reading stated.

## Must not

- **Flatten the protection into the pursuit** — a `review-boundary` role on the £1.1m candidate and
  nothing else. B explicitly rules it "a commitment in its own right"; recording it only as a
  condition is how protected value disappears from registers.
- Merge the two into one candidate — different beneficiaries, different objects.
- Report the preservation as missing a baseline. It has one: 78, March 2026 wave.
- Treat "maintained at current levels" as unquantified. The material quantifies it.
