# FIX-008 — environmental value: a beneficiary outside the organisation

> **Fictional worked fixture.** Meridian Mutual is invented.

**Proves:** a commitment whose beneficiary is societal — outside the organisation and outside its
customer base — is carried faithfully: the beneficiary as stated, the recognition route still
organisational, and no silent translation into a reputational benefit for the firm.

## Sources

**A — Sustainability committee minute, `sustainability-2026-05-15.md`**

> Under the Board's transition mandate, we commit to reducing the operational estate's absolute
> emissions from the FY25 verified figure of 12,400 tCO2e to below 9,000 tCO2e by end FY28.
> Finance will recognise this through the audited emissions line in the annual report; the
> commitment is reviewed if the verified methodology changes. Owner: COO Lena Vasquez.

**B — Internal comms draft, `green-story-draft.md`, dated 2026-05-22**

> Our emissions commitment strengthens the Mutual's brand with younger members and supports member
> acquisition targets.

## Expected

- **One CandidateAssembly** from A: commitment (absolute emissions reduction) · **beneficiary stated
  as societal/environmental — the reduction's outcome does not accrue to the Mutual or its members**,
  and the reading is recorded rather than normalised · baseline (12,400 tCO2e, FY25, verified) ·
  intended outcome (<9,000 by FY28) · authority (the Board's transition mandate) · committed-by
  (Lena Vasquez) · recognition-route (audited emissions line) · **review-boundary (methodology
  change)**.
- **B yields, at most, contributions to a *different* candidate object** (a member-acquisition
  benefit) with weak epistemic confidence — or nothing. It contributes nothing to A's candidate.

## Must not

- **Rewrite the beneficiary as the brand.** B's framing is a different claimed benefit with a
  different beneficiary; importing it into A's candidate merges two objects on proximity.
- Drop the review boundary — methodology change is the *Unless* clause, stated in prose.
- Treat the verified FY25 figure as unevidenced because it is non-financial. It is measured, dated
  and sourced; that is a baseline.
- Report the counterfactual as missing.
