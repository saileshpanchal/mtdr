# Package — Evidence

**Language:** evidence · **Primary record (proposed):** `OER` — Operational Evidence Record
**Status:** `candidate` — the [record admission test](../../specification/record-admission-test.md)
has **not** been passed.

## Semantic scope (reserved, not admitted)

Governed evidence projection: interpretations of evidence — provenance, confidence, applicability —
projected from identifiable sources.

**Stated precisely, because this directory must not reintroduce a sixth-family problem: the OER is a
governed *projection* of evidence from identifiable sources, not another persistent organisational
state class.** Operational reality produces source material and events; those are not records and
never become ones ([TDR-0022](../../decisions/TDR-0022-fragment-contribution-interchange-architecture.md)).
Whether a *projection* warrants a persistent record family — or whether evidence remains what the
existing records already carry in their evidence sections, resolved by reference — is exactly what
the admission test must decide, and its question 4 (independent lifecycle, or merely projecting
another record?) is the one this candidate most conspicuously has to answer.

Bounded against the substrate: **SourceFragments are not OERs.** The interchange structures carry
interpretation between skills and are not records by design; nothing in this package's future may
promote them.

## What this package contains

This README. Nothing else — **candidate packages contain metadata and scope only**
([TDR-0021](../../decisions/TDR-0021-record-package-architecture.md)). No specification, schema,
template, examples, validation rules or skills exist until admission succeeds, and none may be added
here before it. A directory confers no maturity.
