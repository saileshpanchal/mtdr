# FIX-405 — experience is context, not practice

> **Fictional worked fixture.** Hollin Vale Building Society is invented.

**Proves:** what the organisation has learned *about this work* is inherited context; how the work is
normally done is practice. One span can carry both, and must be split.

## Source

**A — Post-incident review, January 2026**

> Three of the last four complaints came from exceptions held over a bank holiday. From now on, clear
> severe exceptions before any bank holiday weekend.

## Expected

- `inherited-context`: *three of the last four complaints came from exceptions held over a bank
  holiday* — a fact about this work.
- `practices`: *clear severe exceptions before a bank holiday weekend* — a default, open to judgement.
- Provenance from the same span on both.

## Must not

- Put the complaint history under **You should**.
- Put the instruction under **You must** — no obligation, policy or control is cited, so it is a
  practice, however firmly worded.
- Drop either half because the span was already used.
