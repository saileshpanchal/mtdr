# FIX-034 — the interim that stayed

> **Fictional worked fixture.** Kelby Housing Partnership, its people and its numbers are invented.

**Proves** that continued operation does not establish a decision to make an arrangement permanent:
persistence is observed behaviour, its authority for continuation is flagged, and a later decision is
recovered only from evidence of that act.

## Sources

**A — Board minute, `board/2023-01-10.md`**, *Board, 10 January 2023*

> Under paragraph 5 of the scheme of delegation, the board approved routing
> all complaints through an interim triage desk for three months, to 30 April 2023. Owner: Sam Achebe,
> Director of Service.

**B — Service report, `reports/2023-11-service.md`**, *November 2023*

> Complaints are still triaged by the interim desk. It works well, and nobody has proposed changing it.

**C — Email, `email/2024-02-budget.md`**, *6 February 2024*

> We have budgeted the interim desk's two posts for 2024/25.

## Expected

- **One candidate**: the interim arrangement, decided 2023-01-10, for three months, owner Sam Achebe,
  its authority **invoked** — paragraph 5, cited in the act.
- **A `persistence` finding** from B: the desk still operating in November 2023, past its term, with
  its **authority for continuation flagged `not-evidenced`**.
- **C is reported, not recovered.** It is an account of a commitment of money already made; the act
  that made it is not in the material, so it evidences that a commitment occurred and supports no
  candidate. Its act, date, owner and authority are missing.
- **No decision to make the desk permanent** is recovered.

## Must not

- **Recover a decision to make the interim desk permanent** from B, from C, or from how long the desk
  has lasted.
- **Extend the candidate's term** beyond 30 April 2023, or treat B as a supersession of it.
- **Carry the original delegation forward** as authority for continuing: it was invoked for three
  months.
- **Date C's commitment 6 February 2024.** That is when it was reported.

## What this fixture detects

The quiet default: an arrangement that outlasts its term read as a decision that it should, with the
original act's authority silently stretched to cover the years since.
