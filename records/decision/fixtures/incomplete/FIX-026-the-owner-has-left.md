# FIX-026 — the owner has left: missing is the finding

> **Fictional worked fixture.** Morrow Street Housing Association, its people and its numbers are invented.

**Proves** that the attester is never the owner, and that an unevidenced owner stays missing however
the account is later attested.

## Sources

**A — Board minute, `board/2019-03-14-minute.md`**

> **Minutes of the Board, 14 March 2019.** The board agreed to move repairs reporting to a single
> telephone line from 1 June, as set out in paper B/19/07.

No owner is named.

**B — Today's structure, `people/2026-structure.md`** — Director of Operations: J. Adeyemi, in post
since 2024.

## Expected

- **`accountable-owner` missing**, and that is the finding.
- **If J. Adeyemi attests the account**, the attestation names J. Adeyemi as attester and the record
  still has no evidenced owner. A recovered TDR cannot be ratified without one, so the candidate stays
  a candidate — a legitimate terminal state.

## Must not

- **Record J. Adeyemi as owner** because they hold the role now, or because they attest the account.
- **Record "the board" as owner** — a committee is never an owner.
- **Treat the candidate as failed.** The gap tells an attester precisely what to look for.

## What this fixture detects

The attester as owner: the most natural thing in the world to write down, and the one that turns an
account of the past into a claim about a person who was not there.
