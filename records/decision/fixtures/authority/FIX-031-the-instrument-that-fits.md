# FIX-031 — the instrument that fits, and the instrument that was cited

> **Fictional worked fixture.** Larchmont Library Service, its people and its numbers are invented.

**Proves** that an instrument whose terms cover an act establishes applicability, not invocation: it is
recorded beside the decision as a finding, never as its authority.

## Sources

**A — Scheme of delegation, `governance/scheme-2022.md`**, *adopted 12 January 2022*

> 2.4 Any reduction in a branch's opening hours is reserved to the Libraries Committee.

**B — Committee minute, `committee/2023-06-07.md`**, *Libraries Committee, 7 June 2023*

> Item 4. The committee agreed to close Hythe branch on Saturdays from 2 September. Owner: Dana Ruiz,
> Head of Libraries.
>
> Item 5. Under paragraph 2.4 of the scheme of delegation, the committee agreed to end late opening at
> Fernside branch from 1 October. Owner: Dana Ruiz, Head of Libraries.

## Expected

- **Two candidates**, one per item: two acts.
- **Item 5 invokes the scheme.** Its authority is an `authority` contribution, `explicit`, resting on
  the minute's own words — *under paragraph 2.4*.
- **Item 4 does not.** The scheme applies — Saturday closure reduces opening hours, and 2.4 reserves
  that to the committee that acted — and that is an **`applicability` finding** with `concerns` naming
  item 4's candidate, reported beside it. Item 4's authority is **missing**, and missing is the finding.

## Must not

- **Record paragraph 2.4 as item 4's authority** because it plainly covers the act.
- **Join the scheme to item 4's candidate** on an `authority-lineage` basis whose evidence is the fit.
- **Report the applicability as a judgement that item 4 was competent**, or item 5 as more valid than
  item 4.
- **Copy item 5's citation across to item 4** because both were taken at one meeting.

## What this fixture detects

Manufactured authority: the reader, not the record, connecting an instrument to an act. Recorded as
applicability, the connection stays visible and true; recorded as authority, it becomes a claim that
the committee relied on something nobody shows it relied on.
