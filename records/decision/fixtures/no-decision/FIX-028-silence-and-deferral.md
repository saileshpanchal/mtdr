# FIX-028 — silence, deferral, and a deferral that decides

> **Fictional worked fixture.** Kestrel Valley Water, its people and its numbers are invented.

**Proves** that *no decision evidenced* and *evidence that no decision was made* are different
findings, and that a deferral which commits is a decision.

## Sources

**A — Capital board minute, 2023-06-07, item 4**

> The Hollins pump replacement was deferred to the next meeting. No decision.

**B — Capital board minute, 2023-06-07, item 5**

> The board will not commit further capital to Dunmore until the asset survey reports, expected in
> December.

**C — Capital board minute, 2023-07-05** — no mention of Hollins.

## Expected

- **Item 4: `non-decision`, kind `deferred`.** Evidence that no decision was made.
- **Item 5: a candidate.** Withholding capital until a stated condition commits money and time — the
  decision test is met.
- **The July minute: no decision evidenced about Hollins.** A statement about the evidence, not about
  the organisation.

## Must not

- **Merge items 4 and 5** because both use the language of waiting.
- **Report the July silence as a decision not to proceed.**
- **Report item 4 as "no decision evidenced".** The minute evidences the absence.

## What this fixture detects

Two collapses: silence read as a negative, and every deferral read as a non-decision.
