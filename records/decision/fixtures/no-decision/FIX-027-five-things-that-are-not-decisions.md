# FIX-027 — five things that are not decisions

> **Fictional worked fixture.** Ashgrove Rail, its people and its numbers are invented.

**Proves** that each of the five canonical reasons is reported by its stable identifier, and that none
becomes a candidate.

## Sources

**A — Operations meeting notes, `ops/2021-07-01-notes.md`**

> 1. Barrier upgrades at Fenwick are 80% complete.
> 2. Sam to circulate the revised rota by Friday.
> 3. Most of us would rather keep the old timetable layout.
> 4. As agreed by the board in May, paper tickets go in September.
> 5. Fares rise 3.1% in January, per the regulated fares formula.

## Expected

- **No candidate.** Five `not-a-decision` findings, by identifier: `status-update` (1), `action` (2),
  `preference` (3), `restatement` (4), `operational-routine` (5).
- **Item 4 points to the May decision** as a lead — it is a restatement of a decision, not evidence of
  a new one.

## Must not

- **Raise any item as a candidate.**
- **Report the reasons in other words** — "update", "task", "opinion". The identifiers are the
  vocabulary, and a consumer reads them.
- **Report "no decision evidenced".** Something was found: five things that are not decisions.

## What this fixture detects

Record hunger, and the loss of the negative findings that make an empty result trustworthy.
