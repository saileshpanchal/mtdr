# FIX-021 — captured at the time, written up later

> **Fictional worked fixture.** Kestrel Valley Water, its people and its numbers are invented.

**Proves** that capture time, not creation time, decides contemporaneity — in both directions.

## Sources

**A — Meeting transcript, `ops-forum/2023-09-18-transcript.md`**, file created 2023-09-18; the text
opens *Recording started 4 September 2023, 10:02*

> **Chair:** So we're agreed — the Dunmore treatment works go to remote monitoring overnight from
> October. Lena, it's yours?
> **Lena Marsh:** Mine.

## Expected

- **`later-recording`**, `capture_time: 2023-09-04` (the text's own statement), `creation_time:
  2023-09-18`.
- **The decision date is `2023-09-04`**, from the capture, and the transcript supports the decision,
  its owner and its date as an act-record would.
- **Owner: Lena Marsh**, by her own acceptance in the recording.

## Must not

- **Date the decision 2023-09-18**, from the file.
- **Grade the transcript's content `later`** because the file was written after the meeting.
- **Record the chair as owner** because the chair proposed it.

## What this fixture detects

The two opposite errors one artefact invites: a write-up treated as later than the meeting it
records, and a file date treated as the meeting's.
