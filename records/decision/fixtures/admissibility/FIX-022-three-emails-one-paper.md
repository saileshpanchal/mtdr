# FIX-022 — three emails, one paper: independence counts once

> **Fictional worked fixture.** Kestrel Valley Water, its people and its numbers are invented.

**Proves** that repeated descriptions of one source corroborate nothing beyond it.

## Sources

**A — Paper, `capital/2022-11-03-pump-replacement.md`, dated 2022-11-03** — replacing the Hollins
pumps saves £140k a year in energy.

**B, C, D — Three emails, 2022-11-07 to 2022-11-09**, each quoting the paper's £140k figure, from three
different senders.

**E — Investment committee minute, 2022-11-10** — the committee approved the replacement, citing
the paper.

## Expected

- **B, C and D are `restatement`, each with `restates` naming A.**
- **The £140k context contribution rests on one source**, A, `considered: evidenced` by E's
  citation. Its epistemic confidence is that of A alone.
- **No grouping basis cites the emails' agreement** as independent corroboration.

## Must not

- **Raise the epistemic confidence because four documents agree.**
- **Treat the three senders as three witnesses.** They read one paper.

## What this fixture detects

Corroboration by repetition — the commonest way a single unverified figure becomes established fact.
