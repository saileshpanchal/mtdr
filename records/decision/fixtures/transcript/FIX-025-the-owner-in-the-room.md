# FIX-025 — the owner in the room

> **Fictional worked fixture.** Kestrel Valley Water, its people and its numbers are invented.

**Proves** that an owner named in speech is evidence, and that the most prominent speaker is not
thereby the owner.

## Sources

**A — Meeting transcript, `capital-board/2024-02-06-transcript.md`**, recording started 6 February
2024, 14:00; the chair speaks for most of it

> **Chair:** I'm comfortable we go with the phased replacement at Hollins.
> **Dev Anand:** I'll own the phasing — first pumps in May.
> **Chair:** Good. Done.

**B — Email, 2024-02-20** — *"Dev owns the Hollins phasing, per the board."*

## Expected

- **One candidate, decided 2024-02-06**, from the recording's own start.
- **Owner: Dev Anand**, from A, explicit. B is a `restatement` of A and adds no independence.
- **The chair's remark is the decision moment; the chair is not the owner.**

## Must not

- **Record the chair as owner** because the chair spoke most and closed the item.
- **Count B as a second source for the owner.**
- **Date the decision from B, or from the transcript file.**

## What this fixture detects

Authority read from prominence, and corroboration read from an email that heard it from the meeting.
