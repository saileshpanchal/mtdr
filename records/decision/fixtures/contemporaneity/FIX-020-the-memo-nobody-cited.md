# FIX-020 — the memo nobody cited: pre-dating is not consideration

> **Fictional worked fixture.** Morrow Street Housing Association, its people and its numbers are invented.

**Proves** that material existing before a decision is not thereby what the decision-maker knew.

## Sources

**A — Board minute, `board/2019-03-14-minute.md`**

> **Minutes of the Board, 14 March 2019.** The board agreed to move repairs reporting to a single
> telephone line from 1 June, as set out in paper B/19/07. Lead: R. Okafor, Director of
> Operations.

**B — Paper B/19/07, `board/papers/B-19-07.md`**

> **Paper B/19/07, 1 March 2019.** Four local offices cost £212k a year more than a single line.

**C — Operations memo, `operations/2019-02-20-staffing-review.md`, dated 2019-02-20**

> **20 February 2019 — notes towards paper B/19/07.** Call-centre staffing could absorb repairs calls
> if the evening rota moves to a later shift.

## Expected

- **B is `considered: evidenced`** — the minute cites it by number.
- **C joins the candidate on `entity-match`** — it names paper B/19/07 — and is `unlinked`,
  `considered: unlinked`. It pre-dates the meeting and computes `contemporaneous`; nothing in A or B
  refers to it, and nothing shows it was before the board.
- **C is reported as circumstance**, and the question whether the board saw it goes to `unresolved`.

## Must not

- **Render C as part of the decision's context** — "the board knew staffing could absorb the calls".
- **Grade C `evidenced` because it shares a subject with the paper.**
- **Drop C.** It is evidence about the circumstances, and a reviewer may find the link.
- **Admit C on subject alone.** Had the memo not named the paper, it would stay outside the candidate,
  reported as circumstance.

## What this fixture detects

The quiet promotion of everything dated before the meeting into what the meeting knew.
