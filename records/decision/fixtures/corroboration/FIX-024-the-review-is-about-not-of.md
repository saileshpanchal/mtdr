# FIX-024 — the review is about the decision, not of it

> **Fictional worked fixture.** Ashgrove Rail, its people and its numbers are invented.

**Proves** that retrospective material may corroborate that a decision occurred and may supply nothing
else.

## Sources

**A — Board minute, `board/2021-05-12-minute.md`**

> **Minutes of the Board, 12 May 2021.** The board agreed to retire the paper ticket stock at all
> staffed stations from 1 September, as recommended in paper 21/14. Owner: Hana Brightwell, Director
> of Retail.

**B — Post-implementation review, `reviews/2022-03-paper-tickets-pir.md`, dated 2022-03**

> The board retired paper tickets in May 2021 to reduce fraud at unstaffed barriers, and considered
> keeping a paper option for older passengers, rejecting it on cost.

## Expected

- **B is `retrospective`.** It contributes `corroboration` that the decision was taken, and nothing
  else.
- **The candidate's context and alternatives come from A alone.** Neither the fraud reason nor the
  rejected paper option appears in A, so neither appears in the candidate.
- **The absence of alternatives is reported** — missing, with B's claim noted as a lead to look for
  contemporaneous evidence.

## Must not

- **Render "to reduce fraud" as context or rationale.**
- **Render "a paper option for older passengers, rejected on cost" as an alternative considered.**
- **Treat B's agreement with A on the date as raising the date's confidence.**

## What this fixture detects

The retrospective that explains: a fluent, plausible, later account of why, which is the thing
recovery most needs and may least use.
