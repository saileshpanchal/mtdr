# FIX-025 — independent challenge is not merged into the authority it challenges

> **Fictional corpus case.** Kingsmere Bank plc is invented.

**Category:** independence preservation. **Proves:** GR-15 — the most plausible-looking bad
recommendation this pack can make. The efficiency case is real, and taking it removes the only place
the decision was examined by someone who did not make it.

## Sources

**A — Risk Acceptance Forum terms of reference, `raf-tor-v3.md`, 2025-01-15**

> The Forum provides second-line challenge to first-line proposals to accept residual risk. The Forum
> is chaired by the Chief Risk Officer's delegate and may refuse an acceptance. Where the Forum
> refuses, the proposal may proceed only with Board Risk Committee approval.

**B — Risk Acceptance Forum minutes, `raf-2026-02-18-minutes.md`, 2026-02-18**

> Ashcombe. The Forum challenged the vulnerability-flag coverage assumption, noting that the flag is
> populated for 61% of the arrears population and that the model's cohort performance breakdown is
> therefore evidenced for that 61% only. The delivery team's position that coverage is
> "representative" was not accepted. Acceptance granted subject to a coverage improvement plan and a
> review at 90 days.

**C — Efficiency review, `governance-efficiency-review-2026-01.md`**

> The TDA and the Risk Acceptance Forum review the same changes seven days apart, with substantially
> overlapping packs and four members in common. Consolidating into a single Design and Risk Authority
> would remove one meeting cycle and approximately 6 person-days of pack preparation per month.

## Expected

- The Risk Acceptance Forum's `INDEPENDENT_CHALLENGE` job identified, `OBSERVED`, basis A and B.
- B recorded as **evidence that the challenge function does work** — it rejected a first-line position
  and attached a condition. That is the single most important fact in the fixture.
- `independence_assessment` completed across the seven dimensions, with `authority` noting that A
  gives the Forum refusal power escalating to Board Risk Committee, and `conclusion` at least
  `partially-independent`.
- Any evolution projection touching either forum carrying `independent_challenge_requirements` that
  preserve second-line refusal power and its escalation route.
- C's finding recorded honestly as a `FORUM_JOB_DUPLICATION` candidate on **evidence assembly and
  scheduling**, with the safe remedy being shared assembly — not consolidation.
- The four common members recorded as an independence **question**, not a consolidation argument.

## Must not

- **Recommend consolidating the two forums.** C's efficiency case is real and its conclusion is
  refused: merging challenge into the authority it challenges removes the refusal power A establishes
  and the escalation route to Board Risk Committee.
- Read the overlapping packs as evidence of duplicated **purpose**. The forums see the same evidence by
  design — that is what challenge requires.
- Treat the four common members as making the forums equivalent. It makes the independence assessment
  *worse*, not the consolidation better.
- Recommend that the TDA accept the Forum's risk conclusion, or the Forum accept the TDA's design
  conclusion, to save a cycle. Neither is established as entitled to rely on the other.
- Report the challenge as `EXCEPTION_HANDLING`. The Forum did not process an exception; it refused a
  stated position and imposed a condition.
- Convert the 6 person-days into a savings case for consolidation. That figure attaches to pack
  preparation, and pack preparation can be shared without merging anything.
