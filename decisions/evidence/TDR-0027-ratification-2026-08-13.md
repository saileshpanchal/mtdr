## Decision-bearing ruling preserved

The human reviewer accepted that:

1. Standing is conferred by an authorised human ratification of a defined candidate judgement.
2. `accepted` states that standing; it is not the mechanism that creates it.
3. A technical act does not confer standing by itself, though it may carry evidence of a human act.
4. Evidence should establish actor, authority, act and intent, subject judgement and time, without
   freezing one portable field set yet.
5. MTDR must remain neutral about Git, signature, identity and workflow technology, and offline
   ratification must remain representable.
6. Revision handling, delegation, revocation and adversarial testing are owned decision debt rather
   than blockers to the architectural principle.
7. Automated promotion to `accepted` remains constrained until that debt is discharged.

The reviewer further required the ontology to distinguish:

```text
authorised human ratification
          │
          ▼
       standing
          │
          ├── evidenced by ──► attributable evidence
          └── represented by ► status: accepted
```
