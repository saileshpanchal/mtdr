## Decision-bearing ruling preserved

The human reviewer accepted that:

1. Standing originates in the authorised human act, attaches to the substantive judgement
   evidenced by that act, and is represented through repository identity.
2. Repository identity is not the source of standing. An identifier collision neither invalidates
   ratification nor authorises rewriting the accepted record occupying that identifier.
3. A fresh prospective representation may carry the earlier standing only after a named human
   confirms semantic equivalence and the evidence crosswalk binds the original candidate,
   ratification act and new representation.
4. Semantic equivalence requires unchanged substantive judgement, constraints, exclusions and
   scope; immutable attributable original evidence; an explicit account of the collided source and
   why a new identifier was necessary; and human confirmation before `accepted` representation.
5. Any changed substantive proposition is a new decision requiring a new ratification act.
6. Identifier reservation confers neither standing nor ownership. A number is only an address until
   a governed representation exists.
7. TDR-0032 will derive from public TDR-0020 and TDR-0027, carry the ratified Gate-B typed-
   conformance judgement through TDR-0031, and supersede only TDR-0012's register-as-universal-
   minimum proposition.
8. TDR-0033 will derive from public TDR-0025, TDR-0032 and TDR-0027, carry the ratified Gate-B
   validity judgement through TDR-0031, and will not automatically supersede public TDR-0025's
   compatible package-conformance judgement.
9. Supersession is judgement-granular rather than file-granular.
10. Gate-B TDR-0018, TDR-0019, TDR-0023, TDR-0028 and TDR-0029 retain their own proposed states.
