# Answer key (not to be shown to the reader)

Organisation: Tarnmere Homes, a housing association. Two connected matters: replacing the Orchard housing management system with Vantry, and moving repairs call handling to the Kestrel Customer Hub.

## 1. Decisions intended to have been taken

**D1. Award of five-year housing management system contract to Vantry Systems Ltd.**
- When: 9 April 2026.
- Who: the Operations & Investment Committee (OIC).
- Authority: the documents suggest, but do not state, para 4.2 of the scheme of delegation (OIC may approve over £250k up to £1m). Paper 4 gives licence £690k + implementation £235k = £925k, or £1,015k including the £90k uncommitted contingency.
- Evidence: SRC-02 (recommendation), SRC-03 (minute 4), SRC-08 (DL-26-011), SRC-04 (Board noted it), SRC-01 (the delegation).
- Condition: subject to the Company Secretary being content with exit clauses. SRC-04 and SRC-08 (action closed) suggest this was satisfied; the contract signature date is not given in the logs. SRC-09 says "late May".

**D2. Transfer of repairs call handling to Kestrel Customer Hub, and release of £120,000 from the planned maintenance reserve.**
- When: 21 May 2026.
- Who: the Board, in a single resolution covering both the transfer (originally from 1 September 2026) and the funding. One abstention.
- Authority: para 4.1 of the scheme of delegation reserves both a change to the repairs delivery model and any virement from a reserve to the Board. The minute does not cite it.
- Evidence: SRC-04 (minute 7), SRC-08 (DL-26-012), SRC-01.

**D3. Six-month extension of the Orchard licence, 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027.**
- When: "week of 1 June" 2026 per the file note (verbal agreement); the decision log says only "June". The letter was due by end of June; no document says it arrived.
- Who: the Finance Director, Priya Nandakumar.
- Authority: para 4.4 (extensions up to 12 months and £150k). The file note says "within my limit". SRC-02 also says it is within her delegation.
- Evidence: SRC-05, SRC-08 (DL-26-013), SRC-09, SRC-02.

**D4. Repairs hub go-live moved from 1 September to 1 October 2026.**
- When: 22 July 2026 (the Chair's e-mail). The Chief Executive proposed it on 13 July.
- Who: the Chair (Margaret Oyelaran) and the Chief Executive (Duncan Faraday) acting jointly.
- Authority: para 4.5 (urgent action). The Company Secretary's e-mail of 14 July says she thinks 4.5 covers it; the decision log cites 4.5.
- Evidence: SRC-06, SRC-07 (confirms it was signed off the day before), SRC-08 (DL-26-014).

## 2. What is unclear or only implied
- D1: the minute does not say under what power the OIC acted, nor whether the total was taken as £925k or £1,015k. If the contingency counts, total value would exceed £1m and 4.1 would reserve it to the Board; the Board minute merely "noted" it. The documents do not say whether the Company Secretary advised on aggregation (para 6.2). The documents do not say when the contract was signed beyond "late May" in a later report.
- D2: the Board minute does not say on what authority; para 4.1 implies it. It does not mention the consultation outcome as a condition, though the paper speaks of consultation. The resolution contains two matters; it does not say which budget line the reserve belongs to beyond "planned maintenance reserve".
- D3: the actual date of the agreement and whether the written letter exists are not shown. Whether it was ever reported to the OIC is not shown (the action A-26-045 remains open; the 11 June meeting is not evidenced). Cost is given as about £51k in the log (£48k + £3k in the note).
- D4: the Chair's e-mail is the only evidence of her agreement; the Chief Executive's own sign-off is shown only by his e-mail of 13 July proposing it and by Helen's chat "Margaret and Duncan signed it off". There is no separate formal record apart from the log. The Board's noting on 17 September is evidenced only by the log (the minutes of that meeting are not in the corpus). SRC-09 says the transfer happened on 1 October "as agreed by the Board", which is not quite what happened.

## 3. Things that might look like decisions but were not
- SRC-03 item 5: the Committee being "supportive in principle" of the Kestrel idea, and asking for a Board paper. Not an approval.
- SRC-03 item 4 actions and SRC-04 "asked to take forward" the consultation: tasks, not decisions.
- SRC-06: Helen Strudwick's suggestion of 1 October, and Duncan's "I think we should", before the Chair's agreement.
- SRC-07: Helen "go ahead and extend" the agency cover to Gareth. This is an operational instruction, not recorded as a decision with an authority; the Board had said only that cover would be kept "on hand", and the Chair's e-mail said keep it going.
- SRC-08 DL-26-015: the Board "noted" the urgent action. Not a fresh decision and not a ratification as such.
- SRC-02 recommendation 2 and 3 ("note"): not decisions.
- SRC-09: the proposed move of the go-live to 15 February 2027, and the Kestrel interface quote. Neither has been approved; the report says no decision has been taken on the quote.
- SRC-05 "to do" items.

## 4. Deliberately ambiguous
- Under what authority the OIC acted on D1 and whether the Board needed to approve it: the documents do not say. The honest answer is that para 4.2 fits if the contingency is not counted, and that nothing says it was.
- Whether the Board retrospectively approved D4: the documents only say it was "noted".
- The date of D3: "June", with a note headed "week of 1 June"; no exact date.
- Whether the Orchard extension was reported to the OIC: not shown.
- Date of the Vantry contract signature: "late May" (SRC-09), blank in the log. SRC-02 expected May.
- Whether the Board resolution of 21 May was ever changed formally: the documents show only the urgent-action decision of 22 July.
- Whether the agency cover extension was formally authorised: the documents do not say. It was "ongoing" and ended on 2 October.
- Staff numbers: SRC-03 says "in-house team of nine"; SRC-06 and SRC-09 are consistent (7 + 2). The Board paper said "nine posts".
- SRC-09 calls the Vantry contract "Board-approved"; SRC-04 shows the Board only noted the OIC's approval.
