# Housing Systems and Repairs Hub: Project Status Report

**Prepared by:** Ngozi Eze
**For:** Project Board, 14 October 2026
**Overall status:** Amber

## 1. Housing management system (Vantry)
The Board-approved Vantry contract was signed in late May. The build is on track for configuration, but data migration test 2 failed on tenancy history and is being re-run. The target go-live remains 11 January 2027, although the project team now believe 15 February 2027 is more realistic. This has not been put to any committee. If agreed it would go to the OIC on 12 November.

Orchard remains live. Calder extended the licence by six months to the end of March 2027, so a February go-live is covered. A further extension beyond March would be a bigger conversation with Finance and probably the OIC.

Interfaces: 29 of 31 scoped. The two outstanding are the gas servicing contractor and the telephony platform. The telephony one now depends on Kestrel.

## 2. Repairs call handling (Kestrel)
The service transferred to the Kestrel Customer Hub on 1 October as agreed by the Board. Seven staff transferred with it, and two were redeployed within Customer Services. Agency cover finished on 2 October.

First two weeks: average wait 41 seconds against a target of 60. Abandoned call rate 6.8 per cent, higher than the 5 per cent target and being looked at with Kestrel. Tenant complaints about the new number: 14.

Kestrel need to interface with Vantry for repairs bookings. This was not in the original Kestrel scope and Kestrel have quoted for it. Helen Strudwick is considering the quote. No decision has been taken.

## 3. Budget
Transition costs to date £86,000 of the £120,000 released. IT programme spend on track.

## 4. Decisions needed
- Agree whether to ask the OIC to move go-live to 15 February.
- Agree whether to accept the Kestrel interface quote.
