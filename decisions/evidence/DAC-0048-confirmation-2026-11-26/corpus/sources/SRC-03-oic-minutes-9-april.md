# Operations & Investment Committee: Minutes

**Date:** 9 April 2026
**Present:** Tobias Wren (Chair), Anita Folorunsho, Raymond Quill, Priya Nandakumar (Finance Director), Duncan Faraday (Chief Executive)
**In attendance:** Sunil Achterberg, Ngozi Eze, Helen Strudwick (Director of Customer Services), Alison Reddy (Company Secretary, minutes)

1. **Apologies and declarations.** Apologies noted. No declarations.

2. **Minutes of 12 February.** Agreed as a correct record.

3. **Matters arising.** The void property dashboard is now live. Closed.

4. **Housing management system replacement (Paper 4).** Sunil Achterberg introduced the paper. Members asked about data migration and about what happens if Vantry is sold or fails. Priya Nandakumar confirmed the money is in the IT capital line. Raymond Quill asked whether the 31 interfaces had all been scoped; Ngozi Eze said 27 had.

   The Committee approved the award to Vantry Systems Ltd as recommended, subject to the Company Secretary being satisfied with the exit and data return clauses.

   Action: Alison Reddy to review the draft contract and report to Tobias Wren by 30 April.
   Action: Ngozi Eze to complete scoping of the remaining interfaces.

5. **Repairs contact handling.** Helen Strudwick outlined an idea to move repairs call handling from the in-house team of nine to the Kestrel Customer Hub, a shared service run by Kestrel Shared Services CIC and used by three other associations. Savings and service hours were discussed. The Committee was supportive in principle and asked that a full paper go to the Board on 21 May.

   Action: Helen Strudwick to take forward the paper for the Board, including staffing implications.

7. **Any other business.** None.

Meeting closed at 16:40. Next meeting 11 June.
