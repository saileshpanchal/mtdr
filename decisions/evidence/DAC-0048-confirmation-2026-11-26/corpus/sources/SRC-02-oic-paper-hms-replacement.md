# Operations & Investment Committee, 9 April 2026
## Paper 4: Housing management system replacement

Authors: Sunil Achterberg (Head of IT), Ngozi Eze (Project Manager)
Dated: 26 March 2026

### Purpose
To seek the Committee's approval to replace the Orchard housing management system.

### Background
Orchard (supplied by Calder Software) has been in use since 2014. Calder will stop supporting the current version when the licence ends on 30 September 2026. Repairs, rents, tenancy and complaints records all sit in Orchard, and there are 31 interfaces to other systems.

### Options considered
- A. Extend Orchard on a rolling basis. Calder have said this is possible only for short periods and at rising cost. Not recommended.
- B. Vantry Housing Platform (Vantry Systems Ltd). Scored 82 out of 100 in evaluation.
- C. Meridian Housing Suite. Scored 74 out of 100.

Evaluation was by a panel of six, including two from Customer Services and one from Finance. Reference visits were made to two housing associations using Vantry.

### Costs (Option B)
| Item | £ |
|---|---|
| Licence, hosting and support, five years | 690,000 |
| Implementation services (call-off) | 235,000 |
| Contingency (held by Finance, not committed) | 90,000 |
| **Programme total including contingency** | **1,015,000** |

Funding is within the approved IT capital line for 2026/27 and 2027/28.

### Timetable
Contract signature in May. Target go-live 11 January 2027. Because go-live falls after the Orchard licence ends, a short extension of Orchard will be needed. This is within the Finance Director's delegation and will be arranged separately.

### Recommendation
The Committee is asked to:
1. approve the award of a five-year contract to Vantry Systems Ltd;
2. note the implementation call-off and contingency;
3. note that the Orchard extension will be arranged by the Finance Director.

### Risks
Data migration from Orchard is the main risk. Vantry's standard exit terms are weaker than we would like and the Company Secretary has not yet reviewed the draft contract.
