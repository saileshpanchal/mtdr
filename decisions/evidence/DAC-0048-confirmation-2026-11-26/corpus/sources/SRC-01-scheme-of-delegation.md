# Tarnmere Homes: Scheme of Delegation (extract)

Version 3, adopted by the Board January 2025. Sections 4 and 6 only.

## 4. Financial and contractual authority

4.1 The following are reserved to the Board:
- any contract or commitment with a total value over £1,000,000 over its term;
- any change to the service delivery model for repairs, rent collection, or complaints handling that alters how tenants make contact with, or receive, that service;
- approval of the annual budget and any virement from a reserve.

4.2 The Operations & Investment Committee (OIC) may approve contracts with a total value over the contract term of more than £250,000 and up to £1,000,000. Above that figure the Committee recommends to the Board.

4.3 The Chief Executive may approve contracts up to £250,000 in total value. The Chief Executive may delegate in writing to Directors up to £100,000.

4.4 The Finance Director may agree extensions or variations to existing contracts of up to 12 months and £150,000 where budget exists. Any such extension is to be reported to the next meeting of the OIC.

4.5 Where an approval would otherwise be given by the Board or a committee and the next meeting is too late to avoid material harm or loss, the Chair and the Chief Executive acting jointly may take the decision. The decision must be recorded in the decision log and reported to the next meeting of the body that would ordinarily have made it.

## 6. Interpretation

6.1 "Total value" means the whole-term cost to the Association of the contract concerned, excluding VAT that can be recovered.

6.2 Where a project comprises more than one contract, the Company Secretary will advise on whether they are to be aggregated for the purposes of section 4.

6.3 Delegations in this scheme are to the post, not the post-holder. A decision taken under delegation should be minuted or logged with the name of the person or body taking it.

6.4 This scheme is reviewed every two years or sooner if the Board asks.
