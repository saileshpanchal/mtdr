# Board of Management: Minutes (extract)

**Date:** 21 May 2026
**Present:** Margaret Oyelaran (Chair), Tobias Wren, Anita Folorunsho, Daniel Osei, Fenella Marsh, Duncan Faraday (Chief Executive)
**In attendance:** Priya Nandakumar, Helen Strudwick, Alison Reddy (minutes)

Items 1 to 5 not reproduced.

**6. Report from the Operations & Investment Committee.** Tobias Wren reported on the meeting of 9 April. The Board noted the Committee's approval of the contract with Vantry Systems Ltd for the new housing management system. Alison Reddy confirmed the exit clauses had been improved and she was content.

**7. Repairs call handling: transfer to Kestrel Customer Hub (Paper 7).** Helen Strudwick presented the paper. The proposal is that from 1 September 2026 the repairs call handling team (nine posts) transfers to the Kestrel Customer Hub, which would take calls from 8am to 8pm on weekdays and 9am to 1pm on Saturdays. Tenants would see a new freephone number and the same repairs process. Savings estimated at £96,000 a year from year two.

Fenella Marsh asked about the effect on staff. Helen Strudwick said consultation would begin in June and affected staff would be offered transfer or redeployment. Daniel Osei raised concern about the quality of service during the changeover. It was agreed that agency cover would be kept on hand.

The Board **RESOLVED** that the repairs call handling service be transferred to the Kestrel Customer Hub with effect from 1 September 2026, and that £120,000 be released from the planned maintenance reserve to meet transition costs. One abstention (Daniel Osei).

Helen Strudwick was asked to take forward the staff consultation and to report to the Board in September.
