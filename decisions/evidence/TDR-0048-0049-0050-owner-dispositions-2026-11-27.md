## The owner's confirmation of item 6, 27 November

Given with the ratification of the four records, verbatim:

> I confirm disposition #6: a partial date wholly before an event may establish chronological
> precedence, but does not by itself prove that the relevant document version was available to or
> considered by the decision-maker. A partial date spanning the event does not establish ordering.

`historical-recovery.md` rule 5 and TDR-0048 E9 now say so. The computed grades are unchanged: a
partial date wholly before the event grades `contemporaneous`, and whether that version was considered
is graded separately, by `considered`.
