# Scheme of Delegation — v4 (extract)

*Brackwell District Council · adopted 2025-04-01 · fictional*

## Part 3 — Officer delegations, Environment and Place

3.1 The Director of Environment and Place may approve operational expenditure to £250,000 within an
approved capital programme line.

3.2 Expenditure above £250,000, or any commitment altering the service footprint in more than one
ward, is reserved to Cabinet.

3.3 Where Cabinet has approved a programme in principle, the Director of Environment and Place is the
committing officer for individual schemes within it, and is accountable for their delivery.

## Part 5 — Financial control

5.2 Any claimed saving above £100,000 per annum must be counter-signed by the Section 151 Officer
before it is reported as a saving in the Medium Term Financial Plan.

5.4 Counter-signature is a personal act of the Section 151 Officer and may not be delegated.
