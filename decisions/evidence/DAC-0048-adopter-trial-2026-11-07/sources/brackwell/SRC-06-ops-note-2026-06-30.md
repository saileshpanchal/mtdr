# Operations note — refuse rounds

*Brackwell District Council · Head of Fleet · 30 June 2026 · fictional*

Since the Southway transfer in April, the two eastern rounds have been running out of Northgate rather
than Calder Road, because the wash bay at Calder Road is not yet commissioned.

This was set up as a temporary measure for April and May. The wash bay commissioning slipped to
September. Rounds have been stable and crews prefer the shorter yard exit at Northgate, so we have not
scheduled a move.

Northgate has been included in the depot maintenance schedule through FY28 on that basis.
