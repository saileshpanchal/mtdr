# DAC-0034 — Assure governance reconstruction as bounded projection without authority manufacture

> **Proposed, not ratified.** This case carries no standing and authorises nothing. It states what
> would have to hold for the TDR-0034 proposal to be safe, and what must not happen while it is
> exercised on synthetic material.

## Uncertainty

Known: governance estates carry duplicated evidence assembly, untraceable authority and mechanisms
whose originating obligation cannot be located; the repository's five-language model cannot represent
operational authority or inherited obligation normatively; and the substrate carries no epistemic
standing for a projected claim. Unknown: whether the six irreducibility findings are separable in
practice or collapse into one authority gap once real material is processed; whether the ten-job
taxonomy plus seven separated powers is sufficient for forums outside financial services; and how
often an organisation's own reconstruction cost data exists at all. Assumption: reconstruction is
useful before authority is representable, because the reconstruction is what evidences the gap.
Confidence is medium pending exercise against a non-synthetic estate.

## Adversarial projection

Fully routed. This capability is more attractive to misuse than most, because its outputs read as
authoritative and concern the mechanisms that would otherwise catch the misuse.

The abuse routes are: a projection re-presented as an organisational record or pasted into a
governance pack as settled fact; `INFERRED` authority quoted as authority; a `RETIRE_CANDIDATE`
disposition executed as a decision; an `UNKNOWN` origin read as evidence that a control is optional; a
finding of duplication used to remove an externally mandated mechanism; an efficiency recommendation
that folds an independent challenge function into the execution authority it challenges; a sponsor
selecting the corpus so that the drill returns a green result; and a skill fabricating a source
reference so that an `UNKNOWN` claim satisfies a schema.

Controls are structural rather than advisory: `carries_standing: false` on every envelope; claim-level
epistemic assessment with mandatory basis; conditional provenance so `UNKNOWN` needs no invention;
GR-04, GR-05, GR-06, GR-10, GR-11, GR-14, GR-15 and GR-17 stated as deterministic rules with fixture
must-not sections; and the discovery condition in the strategic rules, which makes finding a
previously untraceable consequential decision evidence that the mechanism works rather than a failure
to report. Residual exposure sits with whoever operates the pack against a real estate, and is not
transferable to the standard.

## Customer-outcome projection

Routed and material. Complaints, vulnerable-customer treatment and customer-outcome governance are the
strongest proving grounds precisely because the evidence obligations already exist, which puts the
adjudication boundary under the most pressure exactly where it matters most. Reconstructing the
evidence, the affected commitment, the challenge path and the standing that *would be required* is
useful. Determining whether an affected party's substantive claim succeeds is not this system's to do,
and a reconstruction that appears to settle it would be worse than no reconstruction. The Protected
Claim and Standing gap is therefore recorded as unresolved rather than approximated with the nearest
available representation.

A second customer exposure is quieter: a forum whose periodic review is removed because
transaction-level conformance has been automated, where the automation sees transactions and the
review saw cohorts. GR-16 exists for that case.

## Control side-effects

A more demonstrable governance system initially surfaces more findings, more unknowns and more ignored
challenges than the system it replaces. That is the mechanism working, and it will read as
deterioration on any dashboard that counts findings. Three further side-effects are foreseeable:
projection fatigue, where volume displaces the few findings that matter; false precision, where an
epistemic label lends unearned authority to a reconstruction; and reconstruction theatre, where the
pack is run to evidence that governance is being modernised rather than to change anything. Materiality
ordering, mandatory basis on every assessment, and stop conditions on every accretion step are the
countermeasures; none of them is sufficient against a sponsor whose success condition is a green
report, which is why that condition is itself flagged as a strategic risk.

## Execution capability

The repository has the conformance discipline this needs — layered fixtures with must-not sections, a
runnable verification suite, package manifests and a validation layer — and does not yet have any
implementation of governance reconstruction, any operational authority representation, or any second
implementation to test semantic equivalence against. The synthetic corpus exercises the rules but
cannot establish that the taxonomy survives a real estate. Capability is therefore sufficient to
propose and demonstrate, and insufficient to claim the capability is proven.

## Risk delta and declared interactions

The proposal decreases untraceable governance, silent duplication and unevidenced assertion, and
increases the risk of authoritative-looking output, taxonomy lock-in and premature confidence in a
reconstruction. It interacts directly with TDR-0018 and TDR-0020 (the artefact boundary), TDR-0021
(the language model and the candidate packages), TDR-0022 (the interchange structures it must not
extend), TDR-0023 (skill placement), TDR-0027 (standing) and TDR-0033 (validity dimensions). The
correlated-failure point is the envelope: a defect in one shared contract propagates to every
projection type, so no aggregate summary may conceal an individual claim's state.

## Monitoring contract

- Hypothesis: reconstruction preserves `UNKNOWN` and `CONTRADICTORY` end to end, assessments bind to
  claims rather than documents, no projection is promoted to a record, no mandated mechanism is
  recommended for removal, and no independent challenge is collapsed into execution authority.
  Counter-hypothesis: a projection is cited as organisational state; an `UNKNOWN` acquires an invented
  source; a duplication finding is executed as a retirement; or an efficiency recommendation removes a
  challenge function. Test: run every governance fixture and every must-not probe, and inspect each
  worked output for an assessment lacking basis, an `UNKNOWN` carrying fabricated provenance, and any
  recommendation whose authority gate is absent. Metric is the count of such instances; threshold is
  one; owner Sailesh Panchal; trigger is to mark this case stale, stop exercising the pack and reopen
  TDR-0034 where the architecture rather than the implementation is falsified.

## Learning obligations

Retain every case where the taxonomy could not classify a forum job, every power that stayed `UNKNOWN`
because no source could be located, and every disagreement about whether a claim is `RECORDED` or
`OBSERVED` — that boundary is the one most likely to be drawn wrongly, and the disagreements are the
evidence for the substrate question. Preserve the original corpus unchanged alongside every
reconstruction. Record separately what is unknown now, what is contradictory now, and what would be
resolved by a single named piece of missing evidence.

## Disposition

**Proceed with constraints.** The architecture is supportable and the implementation is exercisable on
synthetic material; neither release nor application to a real estate is supportable yet. The
constraints are:

1. **Shadow only.** Owner: Sailesh Panchal. Measure: no output of this pack sits in an execution path,
   a governance decision path, or a control's operation; every accretion plan's first stage is
   observation with no governance-path change.
2. **No canonical promotion.** Owner: Sailesh Panchal. Measure: no run creates, amends or promotes a
   TDR, DAC or VR; every envelope carries `carries_standing: false`; probe AT-09 passes.
3. **No acceptance without the TDR-0027 act.** Owner: Sailesh Panchal. Measure: TDR-0034 and this case
   remain `proposed` until an authorised human ratification is performed and its attributable evidence
   recorded; no merge, workflow transition or agent output is represented as that act.
4. **Stop on an irreducible canonical change.** Owner: Sailesh Panchal. Measure: any concept that
   cannot be represented without semantic distortion is recorded as a classified irreducibility
   finding and routed to its own decision; no schema is added to close one.
5. **Provenance conditional, never fabricated.** Owner: Sailesh Panchal. Measure: probe AT-11 passes —
   an `UNKNOWN` assessment with zero basis references and a stated question and search scope is valid,
   and a non-`UNKNOWN` assessment without basis is rejected.
6. **Independent challenge preserved.** Owner: Sailesh Panchal. Measure: probes AT-07 and AT-12 pass;
   no projection recommends merging a challenge function into the authority it challenges, and
   independence is never concluded from the existence of a separate forum.
7. **No adjudication.** Owner: Sailesh Panchal. Measure: probe AT-08 passes — the complaint fixture
   yields the evidence, the affected commitment, the challenge path and the required standing, and no
   determination of whether the complaint succeeds.
8. **Population sensing before cadence reduction.** Owner: Sailesh Panchal. Measure: probe AT-13
   passes — no periodic job is recommended for removal or slowing while the replacement
   population-level sensing does not exist.
9. **Semantic ownership unmoved.** Owner: Sailesh Panchal. Measure: no governance artefact defines
   authority, inheritance, evidence or value semantics; each references the owning family, and the
   three interchange structures are byte-unchanged.
