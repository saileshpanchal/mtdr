nominal. Any recommendation touching this forum starts there.

## The consolidation proposal

An efficiency review proposes merging this forum with the design authority — overlapping packs, four
common members, ~6 person-days a month. The saving is real. The proposal is **refused**: consolidation
would remove the refusal power the terms of reference confer and the escalation route to Board Risk
Committee, which is the only thing in this estate that survives the accepting function's disagreement.

The saving is available without it. See [`forum-evolution-risk.md`](forum-evolution-risk.md).
