> own templates. **Neither forum's jobs, membership, authority or reporting line changes.**

`new_authority_or_delegation_required` records: *none — no job moves out of either forum, no approval
is delegated, and no reporting line changes.* That is deliberately the whole point.

## The consolidation case, and why it is refused

The efficiency review is right about the facts. The two forums review the same changes seven days
apart, with substantially overlapping packs and four members in common, at roughly 6 person-days of
preparation per month.

Consolidating into a single Design and Risk Authority would remove the second-line **refusal power**
the terms of reference confer, and the **escalation route to Board Risk Committee** that refusal
triggers. On 2026-02-18 that function rejected a first-line coverage assumption and imposed a
condition. Merging challenge into the authority it challenges removes the only place in this estate
where a decision was examined by someone who did not make it.

**The saving does not require the merge.** It is in pack preparation, and pack preparation can be
shared without changing anything else.

## The second finding

The two packs were cut on **2026-02-04** and **2026-02-11**. The two forums assessed the same change
against different evidence vintages. A single cut-off fixes a traceability problem the efficiency
review did not identify.

## Preconditions, success, stop

**Preconditions** — a single cut-off date acceptable to both forums; each forum's template-specific
content identified and preserved; confirmation that shared assembly does not reduce what the second
line sees.

**Success evidence** — preparation effort below the 6 person-day baseline; both forums on the same
evidence vintage; **no reduction in the substance or volume of second-line challenge.**

**Stop conditions** — the second-line forum reports seeing less than before; challenge falls after the
change; a single cut-off proves incompatible with either forum's decision timing.

## What must remain true

- Second-line refusal power, intact.
- Escalation to Board Risk Committee, intact.
- The Forum must not be merged into, or made to rely on the conclusions of, the design authority.
- **Shared evidence assembly must not become shared conclusion.**
- The four common members remain an open independence question, and must not be increased.

## Accountability

The Chief Risk Officer's delegate must be **named**. An unnamed chair is not an accountability
terminus, and that is a precondition of relying on the Forum at all — before any change to how it is
supplied.
