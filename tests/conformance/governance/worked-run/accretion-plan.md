# Governance accretion plan — Kingsmere collections governance

Machine-readable: `accretion-*.json`. **Every step is a proposal.** Nothing here is scheduled,
approved or committed.

```
observe → shadow → measure → reuse → nudge → consolidate → enforce
```

| Phase | Objective | Shadow | Legal review |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 BASELINE** | Establish what the estate is and what it can demonstrate | yes | no |
| **1 OBSERVE** | Surface gaps, duplication, contradictions and unknowns — no governance-path change | yes | **yes** |
| **2 SHADOW** | Precomputed conformance in parallel with the manual dashboard | yes | no |
| **3 REUSE** | Shared evidence assembly consumed by existing forums | yes | no |
| **4 NUDGE** | Precomputed assessment delivered as evidence to the Authority | yes | no |

**No further phase is proposed.** `CONSOLIDATE` requires evidence reuse proven and mandates traced,
and four dispositions in the ledger remain `UNKNOWN` or conditional. `ENFORCE` is not contemplated.

## Two preconditions that block more than they look like they should

**The accountability terminus is unresolved.** Both forum reconstructions stop at the same place, and
one contradiction — two named executives, no allocating instrument — is behind it. It is a hard
precondition of Phase 4, and one artefact would settle it.

**Phase 1 requires legal review.** Not as a downstream approval: complaints evidence, retention,
privilege and personal data are all in scope from the moment findings are recorded, and routing them
later produces a plan that stops where it becomes expensive to change.

## Phases are per forum, not estate-wide

A design authority at `NUDGE` and a complaints forum at `OBSERVE` is a normal plan, and usually a
better one than an estate-wide gate.

## What the sponsor must be told before Phase 1

**The finding count will rise.** A more demonstrable governance system surfaces more findings, more
unknowns and more previously invisible ignored challenges. That is the mechanism working, and it reads
as deterioration on any dashboard that counts findings.

This run is the worked example. It found a consequential decision whose governing obligation is
contradictory, whose accountable owner is contested, whose contact cap has no locatable origin, whose
Standard 7 waiver expired in March, and whose model oversight committee is not demonstrably
independent of the function it oversees. **None of that was created by looking.**

A programme whose success condition is a green report is recorded as a strategic risk in its own
right. The desired condition is the opposite: discovery of a previously untraceable consequential
decision is evidence the mechanism works.

## Start where the evidence already is

The estate's proving grounds are collections, complaints and customer outcomes — all carry evidence
obligations already, so the baseline is measurable and the shadow output is checkable against
something. A plan starting with the newest technology starts where the evidence is thinnest.
