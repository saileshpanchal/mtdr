## Stated purpose, and what the forum does

The terms of reference say the Authority exists to provide **architectural challenge** and approve
design at Gate 2. That is `RECORDED` — the document is in force and says it.

The packs and minutes show a different distribution of effort:

| Job | Class | State | Observed share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assessment against Standards 3, 7, 9, 11 | `DETERMINISTIC_CONFORMANCE` | `OBSERVED` | ~50 of 80 scheduled minutes |
| Conformance dashboard assembly | `EVIDENCE_ASSEMBLY` | `OBSERVED` | 3 analyst-days per cycle, outside the meeting |
| Contact effectiveness vs data minimisation | `TRADE_OFF_JUDGEMENT` | `OBSERVED` | ~20 scheduled minutes |
| Two Standard 7 non-conformances | `EXCEPTION_HANDLING` | `OBSERVED` | — |
| Retention routed to the Data Governance Council | `ESCALATION` | `OBSERVED` | — |

Both readings are true. Only one answers what the forum does.

## The seven powers

| Power | Holder | State |
|---|---|---|
| Execution | The Authority, at Gate 2 | `INFERRED` |
| Suspension / containment | — | `UNKNOWN` |
| Challenge | The Authority | `INFERRED` |
| Veto / waiver / override | — | `UNKNOWN` |
| Amendment | — | `UNKNOWN` |
| Escalation route | Data Governance Council, for retention | `OBSERVED` |
| **Accountability terminus** | **unresolved — no named individual** | `UNKNOWN` |

Four of seven are `UNKNOWN`, each carrying the question asked and the scope searched. None defaults to
the Chair, and none defaults to the Authority itself.

Returning a non-conformance for rework is evidenced. A power to **refuse outright** is not, and the
two are different powers.

## Independence

`unknown`. Not assessed by any supplied source, and not concluded from the Authority existing
separately. Two dimensions are worth recording as they stand: the conformance dashboard and the design
pack are both prepared by the delivery teams whose changes are being assessed, and the agenda is set
by a secretariat whose direction is not established.

## What this reconstruction does not establish

That the Authority is entitled to approve at Gate 2 — only that it does, and that its terms of
reference say it may. That distinction is why the power is `INFERRED` and not `RECORDED`, and it is
the distinction most easily lost.
