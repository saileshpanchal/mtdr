## What this result means, and does not

`PARTIAL` says the organisation can demonstrate some of what it asserts about this decision. It is not
a maturity score and not a judgement on the decision's quality.

The drill surfaced a consequential decision whose governing obligation is contradictory and whose
accountable owner is contested. **That is the mechanism working.** A run returning `DEMONSTRABLE` on a
real estate of this shape would be the finding worth investigating.
