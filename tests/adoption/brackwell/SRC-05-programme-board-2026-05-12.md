# Fleet and Depot Programme Board — notes

*Brackwell District Council · 12 May 2026 · fictional*

**Present:** Director of Environment and Place (chair), Head of Fleet, Head of Property, Finance
Business Partner.

**3. Depot consolidation — site position**

Highways works at Calder Road (CP-118) are running behind. The board agreed that the Northgate depot
will be **retained for vehicle storage** rather than closed, with only Southway and Riverside
consolidating onto Calder Road.

The board noted this reduces the expected annual saving but did not revise the figure at this meeting.
Finance to bring a revised position to the July board.

**5. Early operational data**

Collection round completion times on the two transferred rounds averaged **4% faster** in April
against the same month last year. Head of Fleet cautioned that April included two fewer collection
days than the comparator and that no attribution should be drawn yet.
