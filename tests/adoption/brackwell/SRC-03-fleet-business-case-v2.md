# Fleet and Depot Modernisation — Business Case v2

*Brackwell District Council · Director of Environment and Place · 4 March 2026 · fictional*

## 4. Financial case

Current depot running costs across the three sites were **£1.42m in 2025/26**, measured from the
FY26 revenue outturn (finance ledger, cost centres 4410–4412).

Consolidation onto Calder Road is expected to reduce annual depot running costs to **£0.98m by
2027/28**, a saving of **£440,000 per annum**.

The saving will be recognised in the Medium Term Financial Plan from FY28 and is visible in cost
centre 4410 once the other two centres close.

**Falsifying signal:** if combined running costs across the retained estate exceed £1.15m at the end of
FY27, the saving thesis has not held and the case must be re-examined before FY28 reporting.

Counter-signed for financial purposes: **R. Adeyemi, Section 151 Officer**, 6 March 2026.

## 5. Beneficiaries

The saving accrues to the General Fund and therefore to Brackwell council taxpayers. Residents on the
four affected collection rounds are the beneficiaries of the service improvements in section 6.

## 6. Reconciliation

Benefits to be reconciled against baseline by **31 March 2028**.
