# Cabinet — minute extract

*Brackwell District Council · 18 February 2026 · fictional*

**Item 7 — Fleet and Depot Modernisation**

Cabinet considered the report of the Director of Environment and Place on consolidating the council's
three operational depots onto a single site at Calder Road, together with phased replacement of the
refuse collection fleet.

Members noted the scheme affects collection rounds in four wards.

**Resolved:**

1. That the Fleet and Depot Modernisation programme be approved in principle.
2. That the Director of Environment and Place be authorised to bring forward individual schemes within
   the approved capital programme line.
3. That a further report on the depot site selection be brought to Cabinet before contracts are let.

Councillor J. Whitfield (Portfolio Holder, Environment) moved the recommendation. Carried, 8–3.
