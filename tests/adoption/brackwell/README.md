# Brackwell District Council — Fleet and Depot Modernisation

> **Entirely fictional.** Brackwell District Council does not exist. Every name, figure, date and
> document here is invented for this exercise. Nothing derives from any real organisation, and nothing
> here comes from any private estate.

A small organisational framework of the sort MTDR is meant to be applied to: six documents produced
over five months by people who were not thinking about records, containing decisions nobody labelled
as decisions, a value claim with a baseline, two documents that disagree, an authority that has to be
looked up rather than read off, a commitment that was quietly narrowed, and evidence that is simply
absent.

It is **local government on purpose**. Every other fictional case in this repository is a bank, and a
claim this standard makes repeatedly — that it is an organisational language rather than a banking
ontology — is better demonstrated than asserted. Nothing in the reconstruction should require any
local-government semantics to be added to the standard. If it does, that is the finding.

## How to use it

Point a participant at public MTDR and at this directory, and follow the published instructions for
your runtime ([`skills/packaging/`](../../../skills/packaging/)). Then compare what comes back against
[`FIX-301`](../FIX-301-brackwell-reconstruction.md).

Nothing about TIM, DTA or any private estate is needed, and if any of it appears to be needed, that is
a defect in the public material rather than a gap in the reader's knowledge.
